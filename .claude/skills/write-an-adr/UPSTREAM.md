# Upstream

The test for when a decision deserves a record (hard to reverse,
surprising without context, the result of a real trade-off) follows the
ADR guidance in [mattpocock/skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills)
by Matt Pocock, MIT licence, Copyright (c) 2026 Matt Pocock. The wording
here is rewritten, and the rest of the skill is this repo's own.
